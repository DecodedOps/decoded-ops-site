Ops health score

A quick operations audit you can do yourself. Rate your business across five areas to see where you stand, and where to focus first.

Five areas, each rated one to five. Each row says what a low, middle and high score looks like, and the result shows on the page the moment the fifth answer goes in.

The five areas

Rate each area, one to five.

1 is critical, 5 is excellent. The rows are pre-set to the example from the drawing (2, 3, 3, 4, 1); change any of them and the result updates.

  1. Systems Integration

    Low (1–2): Systems don’t talk to each other. Data is re-entered manually. Mid (3): Some systems connected. Gaps exist but are known. High (4–5): Systems integrated. Data flows automatically between platforms.

  2. Process Documentation

    Low (1–2): No documented processes. Everyone does things differently. Mid (3): Key processes documented but not consistently followed. High (4–5): Processes documented, followed, and regularly reviewed.

  3. Data Quality

    Low (1–2): Data is inconsistent, duplicated, and incomplete. Mid (3): Most data is clean. Some areas need attention. High (4–5): Data is clean, consistent, and governed.

  4. Team & Capability

    Low (1–2): No capacity or skills for technology improvement. Mid (3): Some capability exists. Training happens reactively. High (4–5): Team has skills and capacity. Technology adoption is part of the culture.

  5. Technology Strategy

    Low (1–2): No roadmap. Systems bought reactively. Mid (3): Informal roadmap exists. Some planning in place. High (4–5): Clear technology roadmap aligned to business goals.

What the score means

Four bands, and what each one usually needs.

The total out of 25 puts you in one of four bands. The description is the same one the tool shows with your result.

  1. 5–10out of 25

    Critical: Needs Immediate Attention

    Your operations are under significant strain. Disconnected systems, undocumented processes, and low team capability are creating hidden costs every day. A Clarity Audit, the full operations audit, will give you a prioritised roadmap to address the biggest gaps first.

    Critical

  2. 11–15out of 25

    Fragile: Some Areas Need Work

    You have some strengths, but there are clear gaps that will become more expensive as you grow. The weakest dimensions are your biggest risk: fixing them now is cheaper than waiting.

    Fragile

  3. 16–20out of 25

    Stable: Room to Improve

    Your operations are in reasonable shape. Targeted improvements in your weaker areas will reduce cost and increase resilience. You are well positioned to evaluate new technology investments.

    Stable

  4. 21–25out of 25

    Strong: Well Run Operation

    Your operations are well managed across all dimensions. You are in a strong position to scale, adopt new technology, and focus on strategic growth rather than firefighting.

    Strong
