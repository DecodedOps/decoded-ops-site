Our system is ancient

The platform you bought ten years ago wasn’t built for decoration.

Generic ERP systems and most inventory management software for small businesses are built for businesses that buy and sell the same thing they hold in the warehouse. Decoration doesn’t work like that.

A blank garment becomes a different, finished product the moment it’s printed or embroidered, and most platforms have no clean way to model that.

Talk about your situation See how the audit works
The symptoms

Seven signs the platform wasn’t built for this.

If you recognise three or more of these, the system is the cause. The inventory software was never designed for decoration, so your team works around it every day.

  1. 01

    Decoration BOMs are tracked in a spreadsheet because the platform can’t model them

  2. 02

    Blank and finished stock get confused, or tracked as if they’re the same item

  3. 03

    Supplier artwork lives in email threads, not attached to the order it belongs to

  4. 04

    Print, embroidery, heat-press: every method needs its own workaround

  5. 05

    The vendor’s support team asks what “decoration BOM” even means

  6. 06

    Customisation costs have exceeded what the licence was supposed to save you

  7. 07

    You’ve quietly accepted this is “just how the software is” rather than fixable

Count the ones that apply. Each one is a field the platform never had.

Where the platform runs out

Built to buy and sell. Not to decorate.

Three of my own photographs from client visits this year. Threaded heads, the garment shelf and a mixed stockroom: the blank-to-finished step that a generic platform has no field for.

The fix

Keep the platform. Add the layer it’s missing.

Ripping out an ERP you’ve already paid for and trained staff on is rarely the right answer. The platform stays exactly where it is, and the missing layer sits on top of it, not instead of it.

Layer 1

Your platform, unchanged

Whichever one you run stays as the system of record for orders and accounts

Layer 2

Decoded Works

Decoration BOMs, blank-to-finished mapping, and supplier artwork, modelled properly for the first time

Layer 3

Website, marketplaces, trade portal

Every channel reading the same decoration-aware catalogue, instead of a generic one

The missing layer

The gap the platform was never built for.

The platform you already run holds orders, stock, customers and accounts. Decoration and artwork sit in a gap it was not built for. There are three ways out of it, and only one of them is a new system.

How I help

No rip-and-replace. Just the layer that was missing.

I don’t start by asking which new platform to buy. I start with what your current platform already does well, then build the layer that fills the gap: decoration BOMs, artwork, blank-to-finished stock. Without touching what already works.
