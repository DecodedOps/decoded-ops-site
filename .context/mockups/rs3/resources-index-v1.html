The order

One order, five steps.

The order that makes the resources worth anything. Choose the platform first, and step five is done first.

  1. 01 · Recognise

    Five warning signs

    Read the five signs your systems are holding you back. If three of them sound familiar, carry on down the list.

  2. 02 · Check

    The audit checklist

    Work through the operational audit checklist. It shows what a Clarity Audit covers, and where your own answers go quiet.

  3. 03 · Measure

    Capacity and seasons

    Run the capacity planning and seasonal capacity calculators: real throughput, and where the gap or surplus lands across the year.

  4. 04 · Document

    Method, SOPs, artwork, Sigma

    The Decoded Method, the SOP template, the artwork approval playbook and the Sigma guide: how the business runs, written down.

    ERP selection, last

    Only now the ERP selection playbook: the brief first, every demo scored against it, mine included.
