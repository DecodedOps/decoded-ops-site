  renders just that one `<symbol>` inline, as the plates themselves do. Recommend
  adding both to the shared sprite centrally.

  current page's wording wins for every heading and paragraph, laid out in the
  plate's structure. Two structural consequences, per page below: (a) where the
  plate merges a section's label into its `route-head` eyebrow, the page's own

  eyebrow and the generic label "Related" is the only text not carried.
- **Mockup-only text adopted** (new structural elements with no page counterpart,
